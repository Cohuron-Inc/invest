How the three signals were read

  • Analyst comment. Average 12-month target against the 4 Sep close, the number of analysts behind it, and every rating or target change in the last 60 days. Only sell-side; X commentators' own targets were ignored.
  • Institutional buying. Net share change across Q2 2026 13F filings where an aggregator published it, otherwise the count of holders increasing versus decreasing and named funds that moved. Positions are dated 30 June, so two months stale.
  • Insider buying. Open-market Form 4 purchases in the last 90 days. Option exercises and tax sales excluded. Selling is recorded because it argues against runway.
Reading rule. Upside alone is not runway. A 60% gap to consensus with the CEO selling and funds cutting is a target the Street has not yet lowered. The names ranked first are the ones where the people with the most information are on the same side as the targets.

Fit to the mandate

For medium-to-low risk over one to three years, five of the ten runway names qualify: VST, AVGO, GOOGL, AMZN and SNOW. They combine consensus upside above 20% with institutional accumulation and no binary event inside three months.

RKLB, KURA, ASTS and NBIS hold the most upside and the strongest 13F accumulation, but each is down 25 to 57% from its high with double-digit short interest. They belong in a capped satellite sleeve. KLAR is a bet that a $10M CEO buy knows more than seven downgrades. Size it as such.

What this probe cannot tell you

13F positions are dated 30 June 2026, so every "institutions adding" claim is two months old and may have reversed. MarketBeat's institutional pages carry a CalSTRS data error on at least ten of these names; those rows were excluded and the buyer and seller counts used instead. Fintel and OpenInsider blocked most requests, so insider data leans on Finviz and MarketBeat. Earnings dates marked with a tilde are aggregator estimates. None of the price targets were checked against the broker notes themselves.

Three things would sharpen the next runway probe: build the price layer so upside can be measured against the corpus's own entry dates; pull Q3 13Fs when they land in mid-November; and add short-interest history so a squeeze can be separated from accumulation.
