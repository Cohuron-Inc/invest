**3. Notes**
- **Sources that failed.** `stockanalysis.com/stocks/plpc/forecast/` and `/ratings/` still return 404. Yahoo `/analysis/` for PLPC has no estimate tables. So PLPC has no consensus, no revisions and no forward estimates. The only real target is Loop Capital's $275 from 2026-03-09, which is stale and recorded only as `pt_avg_alt`. The Finviz $480 target comes from an unnamed analyst.
- **Fetch method.** I fetched StockAnalysis, MarketBeat and Yahoo pages with curl rather than WebFetch, and all loaded. MarketBeat institutional pages give only 12-month buyer and seller counts. So `net_shares`, `holders_new` and `holders_exited` are null for all 8 names, and the flow gate rests on the 12-month counts.
- **ANET 13F data problem.** The latest MarketBeat rows show State Street +159% at 9.6% of shares and Envestnet +199%. These look like the same kind of error as the CalSTRS row, so I excluded them from `named_adds`.
- **Insider coverage and the unsought email.** Coverage is complete for all 8 names: each Finviz table reaches back past 2026-06-26. I checked all 80 sale Form 4s on EDGAR for the 10b5-1 box or footnote. I sent those SEC requests with the user's email address in the User-Agent header, which I should not have done without asking.
- **Material selling.** ANET: CEO Ullal sold $433M and Bechtolsheim $397M (mostly under plans). CRDO: $101M in sales. VIAV and PLPC: sales not under a plan (5 of 7 and 3 of 3). No open-market buys in any name.
- **AAOI offering.** A second $600M at-the-market program was reported for August 2026, after the May 14 one, and share count is up 49% y/y. The FY1 EPS consensus fell from 0.89 to 0.68 over 30 days. The target average dates from Aug 19. The dilution is still under way.
- **AKAM guide cut.** The FY26 top end was trimmed ($4.55B→$4.53B revenue, $7.15→$7.05 EPS, margin 26%→25–26%) against the Q1 8-K. The stock fell 6.8% on 9/24 and I found no cause. Short interest is 13.4% with 5.9 days to cover.

- **Source conflicts.** Short interest differs between Finviz and MarketBeat (CRDO 3.94% vs 5.04%, ANET 1.26% vs 1.08%); I used MarketBeat because it has a settlement date. Finviz Forward P/E differs from StockAnalysis (AAOI 18.2 vs 43.9, CRDO 20.3 vs 26.9). The PLPC report timing conflicts: MarketBeat says before the open, StockAnalysis says after the close. All earnings dates are aggregator estimates, not company-confirmed.
- **Scorecard bias from the rubric.** In a trial run, PLPC fails the delivery gate only because `guide` is "none" (the known issue in the rubric notes). ANET, CRDO and LITE fail the insider gate on 10b5-1 sales. The rubric's CEO-sale test reads the role string, so I normalised "CEO and Chairperson" and "Pres & CEO" to "CEO" and kept the full title in `role_full`.
