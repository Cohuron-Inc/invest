**Notes**
- **Blocked sources.** Yahoo `/analysis/` showed "Oops, something went wrong" for DAL, CAT, ISRG and ADUR, so their revisions come from Zacks detailed-estimates. The Yahoo quoteSummary API returned 429. Fintel `/so/us/` returned 403. CNBC returned 403 to WebFetch. Finviz quote pages returned an empty body to curl for SPY and the sector ETFs; SPY, XLY, XLI and XLV figures come from `data/market/2026-09-24/yahoo`, whose 21- and 63-session windows match Finviz Perf Month and Perf Quarter exactly. The MarketBeat `/price-target/` path now redirects (301); its table is on `/forecast/`.
- **What works.** EDGAR Form 4 XML and data.sec.gov work with curl and a contact User-Agent. Every insider trade was checked on EDGAR for the 10b5-1 flag and transaction codes, and `coverage_complete` is true for all six US filers.
- **ADUR has no insider coverage.** It is a Canadian MJDS foreign private issuer (40-F and 6-K, fiscal year ends May 31), so Form 4 does not apply. Insiders file on SEDI, which was not checked. Coverage is null, which means unknown, not verified absence.
- **ADUR dilution.** Two US offerings in FY26, then in June 2026 a US$15.64M offering at $15.20 and a C$9.16M private placement. The first commercial plant (Chemelot, Netherlands) is not due before early 2028. Four to six analysts cover it. `periods_aligned` is set false because StockAnalysis revenue is in CAD and the currency of Zacks EPS is not stated.
- **Material insider selling.** OSCR co-founder Schlosser sold $22.8M outside a 10b5-1 plan (08-27/28). DAL's CEO, President and two EVPs sold about $30M in one post-print window, none under a plan. CAT's CEO sold $26.2M on 08-28.
- **DAL's consensus is below its own guidance.** FY26 EPS consensus of 6.23 sits under the guided $6.50-7.50, and 30-day revisions are down on fuel (WTI about $96). The Q3 print on 10-09 is the next test.
- **NKE's EPS needs care.** The $0.72 GAAP Q4 EPS includes a $0.52 benefit from the expected recovery of IEEPA tariffs, and Q4 gross margin includes about 900bp of it. The record uses $0.20 as the underlying figure. UBS flags a possible miss in Q1 FY27; that print is on 10-01, confirmed.
- **Conflicts between sources.**
  - Short interest: ADUR 12.96% on Finviz, 8.68% on MarketBeat and 11.67% on StockAnalysis (different float definitions).
  - DAL FY26 revenue consensus: 73.40B on StockAnalysis, 66.58B on Zacks (different revenue basis).
  - CAT Q3 date: 10-28 on StockAnalysis, 11-04 on MarketBeat, both estimates.
  - ISRG average target: 476 on StockAnalysis, 509 on MarketBeat (8-point upside gap).
  - ISRG FY1 EBIT of 4.67B looks like non-GAAP against about 3.45B of GAAP operating income over the trailing year, so its operating margin is overstated.
- **Contract detail for the helper.** `forward_metrics.py` reads `forward.periods_aligned` as a raw boolean, not a `{value, source, as_of}` leaf. The records store the raw boolean and keep the evidence in `periods_aligned_evidence`. The contract in `forward-research.md` should say this.
- **Institutions.** MarketBeat buyer and seller counts cover the trailing twelve months, not a quarter-on-quarter change. Net 13F share totals and new/exited holder counts are null because Fintel was blocked. The OSCR and ADUR tables show mostly Q1-26 filings.
