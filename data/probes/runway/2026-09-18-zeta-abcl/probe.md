### Clinical inflection

ABCL635's randomized Phase 2 trial enrolled 92 women. One 600 mg subcutaneous dose reduced moderate-to-severe hot-flash frequency by 8.8 events per day at week four versus 3.5 for placebo. The placebo-adjusted difference was 5.3 events (p<0.001), with an 83% reduction from baseline versus 33% for placebo. There were no serious or severe adverse events and no discontinuations due to adverse events.

The differentiation is potentially once-monthly dosing and avoidance of the liver-monitoring limitations attached to oral small-molecule NK3 competitors. The principal competitors are approved daily oral products such as Astellas's Veozah and Bayer's Lynkuet. ABCL635 has not been compared head-to-head, and a 92-patient four-week Phase 2 study cannot establish commercial superiority or long-term safety.

### Capital and economics

Q2 revenue was only $4.1M, down from $17.1M, against a $55.4M net loss. Forward operating margin, FCF margin, ROIC and ROIC–WACC remain negative or not meaningful. PEG and EV/FCF are not valid valuation tools; ABCL requires a probability-adjusted pipeline valuation.

The balance sheet is funded. Q2 cash and marketable securities exceeded $565M; a subsequent oversubscribed offering raised $200M gross at $9.75 through 17.44M shares and 3.08M pre-funded warrants. Pro forma cash is roughly $765M before fees, plus access to approximately $110M of non-dilutive government funding. The offering added approximately 20.5M potential shares, roughly 6%–7% dilution.

Jazz and Vertex added more than $100M of upfront payments and validate the T-cell-engager platform. Partner-led active programs fell from 44 at year-end to 35 in Q2, which is an offsetting platform-quality warning.
