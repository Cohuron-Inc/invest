How the signals were read

  • Fundamentals. The last print against consensus and whether the guide was raised, held or cut; the direction of estimate revisions over 90 days; cash conversion; the twelve-month case as the company states it; share and gross-margin trend against the two nearest competitors.
  • Flows and positioning. Open-market Form 4 buys and sells in 90 days, exercises and tax sales excluded; the latest 13F cycle's net share change, holder counts and named funds, dated 30 June 2026, so twelve weeks stale; short interest; and how crowded the name is among the corpus accounts, read as a contrarian signal.
  • Price and chart. Close against the average, high and low sell-side targets; reward to the average against risk to the nearest anchor; position against the 50-day and 200-day lines, relative strength against the sector, and whether the last print gap held.
  • Context. The rate, credit, dollar, oil and factor regime and whether the name's duration fits it; whether the name sits inside a live market theme that is accelerating or fading; dated events in three months.
Reading rule. Upside alone is not runway. A 60% gap to consensus with the CEO selling, estimates falling and the chart below its 200-day line is a target the Street has not yet lowered. Four gates decide the tier: upside, institutional flow, insider activity and delivery. The score decides the order inside it.

What this probe cannot tell you

The 13F figures describe 30 June 2026 and are twelve weeks stale. Fintel is blocked, so net share changes and new or exited holder counts are missing for every name, and the flow gate rests on MarketBeat's twelve-month counts. Yahoo's analysis page failed for most names, so revisions come from Zacks and StockAnalysis levels. Forward EBIT, cash flow and capex for FY2 sit behind paywalls, so operating-margin, FCF-growth and return-on-capital checks are uncovered. Almost every earnings date is an aggregator estimate; Micron on 30 Sep, Nike on 1 Oct, UNH on 13 Oct and GE Vernova on 28 Oct are confirmed. Chart reads come from moving-average distances, not a full price series. Prospect, competition, macro fit and narrative are analyst judgments and are written as such. The 49 screened-out names were judged on the Finviz mean target alone. Calibration: of the September verdicts still priced, IONQ is +14% against an expected +2% and QCOM, called no runway, is +15%. Two names is too few to adjust on, but both errors ran the same way, too cautious.
